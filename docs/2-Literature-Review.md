## 2.2 Suggested Improvements

These are the team's proposed improvements. They are outside the current scope (see the Project Proposal) and will be considered after the core system is delivered. Improvements requested by the lecturer are added to the feedback log above and to this table.

| # | Area | Proposed Improvement | Benefit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payments | Online payment of consultation fees | Removes cash handling at the clinic | Team |
| 2 | Care options | Video consultation (telemedicine) | Allows remote visits | Team |
| 3 | Access | Mobile application or Progressive Web App | Easier access for patients | Team |
| 4 | Language | Arabic and English interface | Wider user base | Team |
| 5 | Security | Two-factor authentication for doctors and admins | Stronger protection of medical data | Team |
| 6 | Operations | Waiting list that offers freed slots to other patients | Fewer empty slots after cancellations | Team |
| 7 | Audit | Full audit log of access to medical records | Accountability and privacy compliance | Team |
